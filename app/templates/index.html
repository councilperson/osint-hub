What is OSINT search?

OSINT search is the practice of starting from one identifier — a username, email address or phone number — and checking open sources for the accounts and public details linked to it. An OSINT search tool runs those checks for you, querying hundreds of sources at once instead of one site at a time.

Former names and gone accounts

Earlier names can leave traces. Sources that still expose a former handle, or the remains of a deleted, suspended or banned profile, are reported separately so you can tell a live account from an old one.

Linked profiles

Connections worth investigating. Compare names, links, avatars and bios across results — shared details suggest a connection. Verify it before treating two accounts as the same person.

Honest result classes

Every endpoint returns one of three answers: hit, miss or unresolved. A site that blocks bots is never reported as a false negative — it is marked unresolved so you know the answer is genuinely unknown.

Questions

What it searches, and what it keeps.

What is an OSINT search engine?

A service that takes one identifier — a username, email or phone number — and checks it against a large catalogue of public sources in a single pass, returning the profiles that match along with the evidence for each one.

Is an OSINT search legal?

Searching public pages for information you could have visited yourself is generally lawful. What is not lawful is using it for harassment, discrimination, surveillance or any other unlawful purpose. You are responsible for how you use the results.

How many platforms do you cover?

{{ endpoint_total }} endpoints on your plan across {{ category_count }} categories: social and messaging, creator and media, developer and tech, gaming, commerce and links, and a long tail of forums and wikis. Pro accounts reach all {{ corpus_total }}, including adult sources.

What does the scan actually do?

Each endpoint is a public profile URL built from the handle. The request is a plain GET; the response is compared against the source's own "no such account" markers. Nothing logs in and nothing reads protected content.

How are results ranked?

By category first, then by how the source answered. A hit backed by an explicit profile marker ranks above one inferred from a status code, and unresolved endpoints are never counted as matches.

Can I search by email or phone as well?

Yes. Email and phone queries run the identity checks that apply to them — mailbox and avatar signals, disposable-domain flags — while username queries run the full {{ endpoint_total }}-endpoint sweep.

Do I need a card?

No. The free tier needs no payment details at all. Paid plans are crypto-only — Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana or Tron — and there is no card, PayPal or bank transfer anywhere in the flow.
